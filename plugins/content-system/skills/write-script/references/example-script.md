# Example script (fictional business, structure only)

A made-up roofing company, written in Reg's format to show the shape: hook, body, climax, ending, one
sentence per line. Never reuse its sentences or topic. The owner's own ten scripts are always the better model.

Trust lane (MOFU), about 230 words.

[3-SECOND HOOK / TITLE]
TWO ROOFING QUOTES CAN LOOK THE SAME ON PAPER. ONLY ONE OF THEM IS STILL THERE AFTER THE STORM.
[BODY]
Have you ever held two roofing quotes in your hands and wondered why one of them costs so much more?

Let me tell you what the paper does not show you.

A quote is a promise written in numbers.

But a roof is not built from numbers.

It is built from the decisions a crew makes when nobody is standing at the bottom of the ladder.

Water does not attack the middle of a roof.

It waits at the edges, the valleys and the vents.

It looks for the one place where somebody decided that close enough was good enough.

So ask the cheaper company how they seal the flashing around your chimney.

Ask whether they replace what is underneath, or lay new shingles over old problems.

Ask who will actually be on your roof, and how long that person has been doing this.

If the answers come back fast and vague, you already have your answer.
[CLIMAX]
A cheap roof is not cheaper.

It is a bill that has not arrived yet.

The difference between two quotes is rarely the shingles…

It is the care that nobody can see from the driveway.
[CLIMACTIC ENDING]
So the next time a price looks like a gift, look at it one more time.

Ask the questions the storm will ask later.

Because your roof does not care what you paid.

It only remembers how it was built.
