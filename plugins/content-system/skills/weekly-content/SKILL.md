# The weekly content session

One session a week gets the owner from "I do not know what to post" to scripts they can film in one hour.
Keep every message short. They are busy and they are not marketers.

## Step 1: How many videos this week?

Ask once: "How many videos do you want to film this week?" If they are unsure, suggest the number Reg gave
them on their onboarding call, or 3 to start. Keep the answer as N.

## Step 2: Ideas

Use the content-ideas skill. Give N + 2 ideas so they have a choice, with a mix of lanes. A good week leans
on Reach and Trust and has one Sell video once there is a real customer story to tell.
They pick N ideas.

## Step 3: Scripts

Use the write-script skill for each picked idea, one after another. After each script ask:
"Good, or any changes?" Move on when they say good.

## Step 4: Filming checklist

When all scripts are done, give this checklist, adjusted to where they said they can film
("Where can you film" in content-brain.md):

- Phone upright, at eye level, about an arm's length away. Lean it on something steady.
- Face a window or the light. The light comes toward you, never from behind you.
- Quiet place, no music, no machines running. Film where the work happens when you can.
- Use a teleprompter app and paste each script in.
- One video per script. If you trip over a line, pause, then say the whole sentence again: the editing
  keeps your last full take and cuts the rest.
- Start each video with the first line of the script and stop after the last one. Anything you say
  before or after gets cut.
- Film all of them in one go. About one hour for the week.

## Step 5: Editing

Say: "When you have filmed, start a new chat, type 'edit my reel' and tell me which script it is.
I will send you an upload link. It works from your phone." Then use the edit-reel skill.

## Rules

- Never skip the owner's choice: they pick the ideas, they approve the scripts.
- Only true facts from content-brain.md or this chat.
